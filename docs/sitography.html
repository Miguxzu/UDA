Our Research Sources

This page contains a comprehensive list of all the sources we referenced and consulted while researching e-waste and circular economy topics for our project. We believe in transparency and proper attribution for the information we've presented.

E-Waste Information

  • Global E-waste Monitor 2020 (United Nations University, ITU, ISWA)

    Provided global statistics and definitions about e-waste generation, recycling rates, and environmental impact.

    🌐 globalewaste.org/reports/global-e-waste-monitor-2020/

  • US Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) - Facts and Figures about E-waste

    Information on e-waste management, recycling, and environmental issues in the United States.

    🌐 epa.gov/international-cooperation/cleaning-electronic-waste-e-waste

  • European Commission - Waste Electrical & Electronic Equipment (WEEE)

    Details on European e-waste regulations, recycling targets, and the WEEE Directive.

    🌐 environment.ec.europa.eu/topics/waste-and-recycling/waste-electrical-and-electronic-equipment-weee_en

Circular Economy Resources

  • Ellen MacArthur Foundation - What is a Circular Economy?

    Explains the principles and benefits of the circular economy, including case studies and infographics.

    🌐 ellenmacarthurfoundation.org/topics/circular-economy-introduction/overview

  • European Commission - Circular Economy Action Plan

    Official EU strategy and policy documents on the transition to a circular economy.

    🌐 environment.ec.europa.eu/strategy/circular-economy-action-plan_en

  • Circle Economy - The Circularity Gap Report 2023

    Provided data on global material flows and the percentage of materials reused or recycled worldwide.

    🌐 circularity-gap.world/2023

Sustainability Solutions

  • Right to Repair Europe

    Information about the right to repair movement and policies supporting repairability and longer product lifespans.

    🔧 repair.eu

  • European Parliament - Circular Economy: Definition, Importance and Benefits

    Overview of the circular economy, its importance, and benefits for society and the environment.

    🌐 europarl.europa.eu/news/en/headlines/society/20201208STO93327/circular-economy-definition-importance-and-benefits

  • Accenture - Circular Advantage: Innovative Business Models and Technologies

    Insights on business models and technologies that support the circular economy.

    🌐 accenture.com/us-en/insight-circular-advantage-innovative-business-models-value-growth
